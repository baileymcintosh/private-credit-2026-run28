## Covenant Quality Narrative

This sharp increase is primarily associated with the substantial dry powder held by private equity firms, projected to exceed $0.5 trillion by 2030. Such capital reserves have intensified competition among lenders, compelling them to offer more borrower-friendly terms, including cov-lite structures.

A cov-lite loan typically relaxes the maintenance covenants, which are financial covenants intended to protect lenders by requiring certain financial conditions to be met by the borrower. The expansion of EBITDA add-backs — where expected cost synergies and earnings enhancements are added back to EBITDA — has further facilitated this trend. With synergies included in 88% of deals and 36-month realization windows, leverage covenants are increasingly difficult to trigger, providing borrowers more leeway and reducing the lenders' protective barriers.

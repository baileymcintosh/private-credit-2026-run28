As of 2024, reports indicated that the market had amassed assets under management (AUM) nearing USD 1 trillion, and projections into 2026 anticipated continued growth due to demands for more bespoke and flexible lending solutions that bank-driven markets cannot provide as efficiently.

**Key Structures:**
1. **Direct Lending:** Typically involves non-bank institutions providing loans directly to middle-market companies. The spreads often over SOFR (Secured Overnight Financing Rate) reflect risk premiums and typically hover around SOFR+500 basis points, varying by borrower credit quality.

2. **Collateralized Loan Obligations (CLOs):** These are structured financial products backed by a pool of loans. Their popularity soared owing to their tranching structure which offers risk and return diversity. After peaking in 2021, AAA tranches were yielding spreads significantly higher in 2026, trending due to augmented risk perceptions.

3. **NAV Facilities:** These facilities remain crucial for private funds, using interests in a fund’s NAV as collateral, which spurred debate due to risks these structures pose if asset values plummet.

4. **Preferred Equity:** Utilized as a lifeline for stressed borrowers; preferred equity provides companies with balance sheet flexibility while being subordinate to debt but senior to common equity.
